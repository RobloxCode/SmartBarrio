# SmartBarrio

---

## Problematic & Challenge Context

Small and medium-sized local businesses—such as convenient stores, coffee shops, boutiques, and workshops—often lack access to modern technology infrastructure due to high costs and operational complexity. 

Under the **InnovaTecNM 2026** framework (Reto 2: Tecnologías Emergentes)[cite: 1], this project focuses on democratizing advanced technological infrastructure by developing low-cost, scalable solutions to digitize, automate, and optimize everyday local business operations[cite: 1].

### Key Challenge Objectives
- **Transform Commercial Spaces:** Improve customer experience, operational efficiency, and energy sustainability for local SMEs[cite: 1].
- **Sovereignty & Low-Cost Prototyping:** Build accessible solutions using embedded systems, IoT, and artificial intelligence without reliance on expensive foreign platforms[cite: 1].

---

##  Our Solution

*the problematic is to transform small business by using smart spaces, either by reducing electricity billing, improving security, or enhancing user experience*

**SmartBarrio** is a ecosystem that operates in both the user side and the business owner side too, first, for the costumer side we will give you an
app where, you as a costumer can loop up any product and the app will show you the nearby shops where you can find that product. This app will take 
many local/small business to be part of it, they will all be on the app.
Now as the business owner side we offer you, a inventory management platform, we also give you some cameras that can scan suspicious behavior in 
costumers, this to make better security.

---

## Key Technological Pillars

Our implementation addresses core focus areas outlined in the challenge[cite: 1]:

-  **Artificial Intelligence & Personalization:** Customer segmentation, demand/offer personalization, and consumption tracking[cite: 1].
-  **Smart Automation & Control (Domotics):** Automated device control, energy monitoring, and environmental adaptability[cite: 1].
-  **Edge Computing:** Low-latency, local data processing inside the business establishment[cite: 1].
-  **IoT & Sensor Networks:** Real-time monitoring (e.g., environmental parameters, cold chain integrity, access control)[cite: 1].
